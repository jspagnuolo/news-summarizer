- Positive cultural exchanges are emphasized by both sides, with Taiwan's audiences responding well to Chinese media productions.
- China asserts that Taiwan's future should be decided by all Chinese people, reflecting its stance on sovereignty.
- Taiwan's government is criticized by China for its educational policies that allegedly marginalize traditional Chinese culture.
- Taiwan seeks international support, particularly from the US, for its participation in global forums and initiatives.
- Concerns over defense spending politicization are raised in Taiwan, indicating internal debates about security and military priorities.
